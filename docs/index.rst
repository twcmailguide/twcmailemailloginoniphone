How to Login Using the iPhone Mail App
======================================

Follow these general steps:

Open the **Settings** application.

Select **Apps**.

Tap **Mail**.

Choose **Mail Accounts**.

Select **Add Account**.

Choose your email provider or select the option to add another mail account manually.

Enter:

* Your email address.
* Your password.

If prompted, enter the correct incoming and outgoing mail server settings.

Save the account.

Open the Mail app to access your inbox.

How to Login Through a Web Browser
==================================

You can also access your TWC Mail account using a mobile browser.

Enter:

* Your email address.
* Your password.

Select the option to sign in.

If your credentials are correct, your inbox will load successfully.
